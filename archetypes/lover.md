# Lover
[Back to this section](README.md) · [Home](../README.md) · Brennan Dahmen

## What is it?
The Lover values connection, appreciation, intimacy, and a rich sensory experience.

## How do you recognize or use it?
Two cups, warm color, careful spacing, and language about time together make the audience's relationship central. Use this approach for gifts, shared experiences, and products that support attention to another person. Avoid implying that a purchase proves love or repairs a relationship.

## Examples
Both designs use the same fictional brand, LITTLE RITUAL, offering free printable conversation cards for two. These are static mockups.

### Example 1: Modernist
<img src="../assets/archetype/Lover/lover-hero-1.png" alt="Lover hero 1: Mid-Century treatment for LITTLE RITUAL, with the headline For people who love slow evenings. and a Get the cards button" width="640">

Archetype: Lover
Style: Mid-Century — [Saul Bass, Anatomy of a Murder (SFMOMA)](https://www.sfmoma.org/artwork/2000.514/)
Persuasion: [Liking](../persuasion/liking.md)
Headline: For people who love slow evenings.

Supporting copy: We love good tea and unhurried conversation, too. Make a little time for each other.

CTA: Get the cards — downloads the free cards, with no sign-up required.

The two cups and unhurried tone support Lover's emphasis on attention and connection. Liking appears when the brand explicitly shares the audience's love of tea and slow conversation. Reduced shapes and open space adapt the modernist economy of the Bass reference.

Archetype: Lover
Style: Push Pin — [Seymour Chwast, End Bad Breath (Poster House)](https://collection.posterhouse.org/poster/end-bad-breath/)
Persuasion: [Liking](../persuasion/liking.md)
Headline: For people who love slow evenings.

Supporting copy: We love good tea and unhurried conversation, too. Make a little time for each other.

CTA: Get the cards — downloads the free cards, with no sign-up required.

The shared-preference copy preserves Liking while the paired cups and heart-like steam maintain Lover. Decorative type, outlined illustration, and historical ornament adapt the eclectic Push Pin approach. The pale text panel separates the action from the more elaborate drawing.
