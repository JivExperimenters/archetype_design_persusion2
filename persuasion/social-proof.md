# Social Proof
[Back to this section](README.md) · [Home](../README.md)

## What is it?
Social proof is the idea that people are more likely to trust or follow a decision when they see that others have already done it. In design and marketing, it means showing that customers, users, or audiences are engaging with a product, service, or idea. This can reduce uncertainty and make a choice feel safer.

The principle is commonly associated with Robert Cialdini. When people are unsure, they look to the behavior of others as a signal. Reviews, testimonials, popularity counts, case studies, and visible usage all deliver this message quickly.

## How do you recognize or use it?
Social proof is easy to recognize in customer reviews, star ratings, “10,000+ users,” or testimonials from real customers. It is especially effective when the proof feels specific and relevant: a detailed quote, a product rating, a case study, or evidence that a result was achieved.

The most persuasive examples are genuine and transparent. If a platform uses fake or vague proof, the effect is weak and can harm trust. Good social proof is specific, credible, and easy for the audience to verify.

### How to use it in a hero
Use a short testimonial, rating, or user count in the hero section with a clear message like “Loved by 2,500+ creators” or “Rated 4.9/5 by users.” Keep the proof easy to scan and pair it with a strong action, such as a button that leads to a sign-up or product page.

## Examples
### Customer reviews
An ecommerce page shows a 4.8-star average rating and a few short customer quotes. The rating gives quick reassurance and reduces hesitation before purchase.

### Case study or success story
A business site includes a short case study that explains the challenge, the solution, and the result. This helps visitors imagine what success could look like for them.

### Visible engagement
A product page highlights “Used by teams at 300+ companies” or “Featured in 25+ publications.” This communicates momentum and makes the brand feel established.
