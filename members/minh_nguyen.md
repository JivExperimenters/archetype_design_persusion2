## About me

My personal archetype is the Magician: innovation as a way to create spectacle and amusement for others. Asking why I want an archetype, how others might see me, and what I am creating for helped clarify that I want my work to surprise, delight, and make an impression.

**Visual direction**

- Pair legible serif and sans-serif typefaces.
- Use neutral colors that still feel lively, with a warm, distinctive accent.
- Keep the voice grounded and accepting: “It is what it is.”

**How to build trust**

Give away useful tools and ideas as an act of reciprocity. Consistently deliver value, then let social proof demonstrate the quality and reliability of that work.

## What I learned
Working on separate files for each topic kept our merges simple, and the shared index files were the one place we had to coordinate. Researching the style pages also showed me the difference between a museum collection record and an image found through search: the record gives the creator, date, institution and reuse terms that a citation needs.
